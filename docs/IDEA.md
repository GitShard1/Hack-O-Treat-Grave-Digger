# Idea: Grave Digger

> Full detail: [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md)

## Hackathon theme / track
"Hack for a Halloween Character": a project a Halloween character could use day to day. The organizer allows a loose reading, so we have to sell it.

## Problem
Every repo has a personality: its languages, its pile of dependencies, its long-silent last commit. Nobody gets to *meet* that personality.

## Solution
Paste a public GitHub URL. The Grave Digger digs up real repo facts and summons that repo's Halloween monster: a procedural SVG character, three traits that each show the fact behind them, and a short fictional origin story.

## Target users
Developers (and judges) who want a fun, shareable portrait of their project.

## Why this wins
- Understandable in 10 seconds, with a fun reveal.
- Every trait is backed by a real, inspectable repo fact, so it isn't generic spooky filler.
- Reliable demo: instant procedural art and a deterministic fallback.

## Ideas parking lot
- Downloadable character card (PNG)
- Saved-example gallery (clearly labelled)
- One-line "talk to your monster"
- Optional AI-painted portrait (never in the critical path)
